## Our Supply Chain Page

### 3-Step Supply Chain Model

1. **Direct Government Sourcing** — Procures fertilizers directly from official government depots and godowns, eliminating counterfeit risk and ensuring compliance with national quality standards.
2. **Secure Transportation & Storage** — Products travel in dedicated, covered vehicles to prevent weather damage; stored in modern, climate-controlled godowns protecting against moisture.
3. **Retail Distribution** — Fertilizers reach farmers through a network of pre-verified, authorized local retailers within the operational zone, ensuring consistent supply and localized farmer support.

### Facilities & Operations
- **Storage:** Modern, secure godowns with optimal condition controls
- **Transportation:** Dedicated covered vehicles for product protection
- **Distribution Network:** Pre-verified retail partners in specific service areas
